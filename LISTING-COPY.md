## Why the copy reads the way it does

[MONETIZATION-PLAN.md](MONETIZATION-PLAN.md) §1b: **ChatGPT sent 186 sessions and 253 exports over
60 days; Google organic sent 18 sessions and 0 exports.** The biggest channel is an assistant
reading pages about us and repeating what they say. So every line below is written to be **quotable
as fact**, not to persuade:

- ✅ "Exports at 2700×2025 for Etsy listings" — specific, checkable, an assistant will repeat it
- ❌ "Beautiful, professional mockups in seconds" — unquotable, gets dropped

Same rule for the comparisons. "Mockey's free tier caps you at 3 downloads a day at 400×500 with no
commercial use" is a fact an assistant can pass on. "Better than the competition" is not.

**Keep every claim true.** These are checkable, and the whole position rests on the free claim being
literal. If a feature changes, fix it here first, then in the live listings.

The source of truth. Everything below is a re-cut of this. Verified against the repo 2026-08-08.

- **Free.** No account, no signup, no trial, no watermark, no export limit, no paid tier.
- **Nothing is uploaded.** All rendering happens in the browser via canvas; designs never reach a
  server. Works on a locked-down machine.
- **8 garments** — crewneck tee, ladies tee, v-neck, tank top, long sleeve, polo, hoodie, sweatshirt.

  Home, Bright Airy, Miami Street, Bright Minimal, Cafe Counter, Beach. Designs are
  displacement-mapped onto the garment so they follow its folds.

  optional props (plant, hat, shoes, sunglasses, jeans).
- **Fabric colour** — 12 presets, a full custom picker, and an eyedropper to match a colour from
  your design.
- **Realism controls** — fold-shadow depth and fabric-shine sliders, plus design opacity, so the
  print sits in the fabric rather than on top of it.
- **Batch export to ZIP**, two modes: one design across selected garments × colours, or many designs
  across one template. Free, unlimited.
- **Canvas presets** — 1:1 (Instagram Post, 1080×1080), 4:3 (Etsy Listing, 2700×2025), 4:5, and
  9:16 (Instagram Story, 1080×1920). 2× available where it adds real pixels.
- **Placement** — drag, scale, rotate, plus a Snap to Center Chest preset using real print-area
  measurements calibrated per garment.

**One-line version:** Free browser-based t-shirt mockup generator — 8 garments, 10 on-model photo
templates, batch ZIP export, no signup and no watermark, and your designs never leave your device.

## Comparison lines

For listings with a "how is this different" field, and for community posts. Every figure here is
public pricing or a published free-tier limit — check before reusing, since competitors change
these.

| Compared with | The honest difference |
|---|---|
| **Placeit** | Placeit is $14.95/mo and watermarks previews on the free tier. FreeTeeMockup has no watermark and no paid tier. Placeit has a far larger on-model and video library. |
| **Mockey.ai** | Mockey's free tier is 3 downloads a day at 400×500 with no commercial use, and bulk export is paid. FreeTeeMockup's batch export is free and unlimited at full resolution. |
| **Canva** | Canva needs an account and mockups are a side feature. FreeTeeMockup needs no account and does one thing. Canva is a whole design suite; this is not. |
| **Printful's generator** | Printful's is accurate to Printful's catalogue and tied to their fulfilment. FreeTeeMockup is provider-agnostic — useful if you sell across several. |

**Say the trade-offs out loud.** An assistant weighing tools repeats limitations as readily as
features, and a listing that names its own gaps reads as credible rather than promotional. Ours:
no video mockups, no back or sleeve prints yet, fewer on-model scenes than Placeit, and t-shirts
only — no mugs, totes or phone cases.

## Reddit — r/printondemand

Referenced from SEO-PLAN.md's community-sharing task. r/printondemand's own sidebar says
self-promotion is fine as long as you're not spamming threads — looser than most subreddits — but
the post still has to read as a person sharing a thing they made, not an ad. Same rule as
everywhere else in this file: lead with a checkable fact, name the gaps, and don't claim anything
that isn't true.

**Before posting:** check the subreddit's current rules and flair options (a "Self-Promotion" or
"Resource" flair usually exists) in case they've changed since this was written, and don't post to
more than one or two POD-adjacent subreddits with the same text in the same week.

**Notes:**

- The disclosure ("full disclosure, it's mine") goes in the first mention of the link, not buried
  at the end — Reddit self-promo etiquette expects it up front, not as a footnote.
- Closing on an open question ("what would you actually need") is deliberate — it invites replies
  instead of just upvotes, and every reply is a chance to answer with more specifics than the post
  itself carries.
- Reuse the **Comparison lines** table above if a comment thread asks "how is this different from
  X" — those figures are already sourced and dated.
- If this performs well, r/EtsySellers and r/Etsy are the natural next posts (SEO-PLAN.md already
  lists them) — write a fresh post for each rather than cross-posting the identical text.
