## The one thing to know before sending anything

Sites like Good Mockups, Mockup World and Unblast are **file aggregators**: each post is a
downloadable PSD/PNG with a preview and a link back to the author. They do not have a slot for
"a web tool". A bare "please feature my generator" email is the one they get most and act on
least.

The fix is to give them a file. Export a **free downloadable pack** from the tool itself — e.g.
"8 Blank T-Shirt Flat-Lay Mockups (PNG, transparent + wood)" or "10 On-Model T-Shirt Mockups,
2700×2025" — host it on freeteemockup.com behind a page that says *made with the free editor,
open it here to change the colour / add your design*. Submit the pack as the freebie. The
aggregator gets what its format needs, the post links to your domain, and every download is a
person who sees the editor. Same pack works for every Tier 1 and Tier 2 site below.

Tier 3 (tool directories) and Tier 4 (roundup articles) accept the tool as-is.

## Sending notes

- One site per email, one ask per email. Do not CC.
- Use a real name and a freeteemockup.com address; the Gmail address gets filtered on these sites.
- Follow up once, 7–10 days later, with a one-line reply on the same thread. Then stop.
- Track in a sheet: site, URL submitted to, date, status, live URL. Ten sent and tracked beats
  forty sprayed.
- Keep every number in the emails in sync with the fact block in [LISTING-COPY.md](LISTING-COPY.md).
