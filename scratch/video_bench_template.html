What actually changed

The first cut ran a hand-rolled hue key in the browser, live, inside a hardcoded torso rectangle. All three faults follow from that one decision: a sleeve swinging outside the rectangle kept its violet, the print was pinned to the canvas rather than to the body, and a key that simple has nothing to say about hair. This version runs the repo's real analysis (scratch/lib/onmodel-analyze.js, now shared with the still baker rather than copied) over every frame, offline. Three clip-specific additions sit on top: one calibration for the whole loop so the colour can't breathe, a print quad re-measured per frame and smoothed over time, and frames resampled to the resolution the analysis's own constants were tuned at.

One thing this found that you didn't report

A hairline of green traces the garment's silhouette — the mint cast the still pipeline fails a build over, left where modelled violet is subtracted from a pixel that is mostly background. It covers __GREEN__% of the boundary band, peaks at __GREENMAX__/255, and is one pixel wide: invisible at native size, and I could only see it at 11× magnification. I have not fixed it, because the obvious fix — a despill clamp on the band — needs validating across every garment colour before it goes near the composite, and a fix I can't check is worse than a number you know about. Sleeve edges against the potted plant are where to look first if it ever does show.
